Stroke rehabilitation at home in Ipoh

After a stroke, movement, balance and everyday activities can change. Nur Siti Eissya offers individual home physiotherapy in Ipoh for people with ongoing movement and mobility needs, alongside their medical care and other rehabilitation services.

What physiotherapy may address

An assessment may identify difficulty with turning in bed, moving between bed and chair, standing, walking, balance, strength or coordination. The plan is based on the person's current abilities, goals, medical guidance and safety needs. Not every exercise is suitable for every person.

What a home session can involve

Where appropriate, sessions may include supported movement practice, transfers, walking or balance work, functional exercises and a manageable home programme. Eissya checks progress against practical goals and adjusts the plan. Progress varies from person to person and no specific recovery outcome is guaranteed.

How families can help

With the stroke survivor's agreement, family members can share daily challenges, join goal-setting and learn how to support agreed exercises or transfers. Caregivers should not attempt techniques that have not been demonstrated for that person.

Part of wider stroke care

Physiotherapy addresses movement and physical function. Occupational therapy may focus on daily activities and adaptations; speech and language therapy addresses communication and swallowing; medical teams manage diagnosis, medicines and other health needs. Eissya can recommend medical assessment or referral when a concern is outside the scope of a home physiotherapy session.

When urgent medical care matters

New facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty or other sudden neurological symptoms need emergency medical assessment. Do not wait for a physiotherapy appointment. In Malaysia, call 999 for an emergency.

Questions families ask

How often should a stroke survivor have physiotherapy?

The right frequency depends on medical stability, goals, fatigue, support at home and the rehabilitation plan. It should be decided after assessment and, where relevant, with the wider care team.

Can rehabilitation continue after hospital discharge?

Home physiotherapy may support ongoing movement goals after discharge when the person is medically stable and home treatment is suitable.

Can someone who cannot walk independently receive a visit?

You can enquire. Eissya needs to understand the person's current abilities, transfer needs and medical guidance before confirming whether a home visit is appropriate.
