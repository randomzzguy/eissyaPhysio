Home physiotherapy pricing

Choose a single session or a package after discussing your needs with Eissya. All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) and apply to home visits within Ipoh.

Ask Eissya on WhatsAppView prices

Single Session — RM140

One home physiotherapy session: RM140, plus a transport fee based on your location within Ipoh.

Trial Pack — RM400

Three home physiotherapy sessions: RM400 total, approximately RM133.33 per session, plus transport fees.

Progress Pack — RM1,000

Eight home physiotherapy sessions: RM1,000 total, RM125 per session, plus transport fees.

Transport fees

Transport fees vary depending on your location within Ipoh. The exact fee will be confirmed with you through WhatsApp before your appointment is booked. There is no fixed transport rate advertised.

Choosing a package

The number and frequency of sessions depend on your goals and assessment. You can ask Eissya about the options before committing to a package.

Questions families ask

Is the transport fee included?

No. It varies by travel distance within Ipoh and is confirmed before booking.

Can I book just one session?

Yes. A single home visit is RM140 plus the confirmed transport fee.
