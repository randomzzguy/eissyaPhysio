Home physiotherapy in Ipoh

One-to-one assessment and physiotherapy in your own home with Nur Siti Eissya. Home visits can make care easier to access when travel is difficult, especially for older adults and people rebuilding mobility after a stroke.

Who may benefit from a home visit?

Home physiotherapy may suit an older parent who finds clinic travel tiring, someone living with limited mobility, a stroke survivor continuing rehabilitation after medical care, or a person managing a musculoskeletal concern. Suitability depends on an individual assessment and medical circumstances.

What happens during a visit?

Eissya first discusses your concerns, relevant history and goals, then assesses movement and function. Depending on the findings, a session may include movement practice, exercise guidance, balance or walking work, and a plan to practise safely between visits. A family caregiver can join with the patient's agreement.

A familiar setting for practical goals

Working at home allows the session to focus on everyday tasks such as getting up from a chair, walking through the home or using steps, when these are appropriate goals. Eissya can adapt the plan as progress and needs change.

Coverage and price

Visits are available within Ipoh, Perak only. A single session is RM140. The Trial Pack is three sessions for RM400; the Progress Pack is eight sessions for RM1,000. Transport fees vary by distance within Ipoh and the exact fee is confirmed before booking.

Questions families ask

Do I need a doctor's referral?

You can enquire directly. If you have recently been discharged from hospital or have a complex condition, share relevant medical guidance during the assessment; Eissya may recommend medical review before treatment.

Can a family member arrange the visit?

Yes. A family member may make the first enquiry and help coordinate the visit, with the patient's involvement and consent.

Which parts of Perak are covered?

Home visits are offered within Ipoh only. Send your Ipoh location to confirm availability and the transport fee.
