Meet Nur Siti Eissya

Eissya Physio is an independent home physiotherapy service in Ipoh. Patients and families work directly with Nur Siti Eissya, the same physiotherapist throughout their care.

Ask Eissya on WhatsAppView prices

Education and clinical background

Eissya holds a BSc (Hons) in Physiotherapy from INTI International University. She has approximately 1.5 years of clinical experience across musculoskeletal, geriatric, neurological and pain-related concerns. She previously worked at Prime Motion Physio Centre in Ipoh from March 2025 to July 2026.

Her approach

Eissya starts by listening to what matters in daily life, assessing movement and agreeing on realistic goals. A home visit lets her work in the setting where patients carry out many of their everyday activities. Plans are adjusted to individual needs and progress.

Communication

Eissya is fluent in English and Bahasa Malaysia. She has basic conversational Mandarin and Cantonese. Families are welcome to help communicate preferences with the patient's agreement.

Where she works

Eissya offers home visits within Ipoh, Perak. She does not operate a walk-in clinic or accept home-visit bookings outside Ipoh.
