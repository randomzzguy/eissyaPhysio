How a home physiotherapy visit works

The first step is a WhatsApp enquiry. Share the patient's location within Ipoh and a short description of the movement or rehabilitation concern. Eissya will discuss suitability and availability before a visit is arranged.

Ask Eissya on WhatsAppView prices

1. Send a simple enquiry

A name, Ipoh location, broad concern and preferred timing are enough to start. You do not need to send detailed medical records through the first WhatsApp message.

2. Confirm the visit and full price

Eissya will discuss the type of support needed, confirm whether a home visit is suitable, agree on a time and tell you the transport fee for your location before booking.

3. Prepare a comfortable space

Choose an area where the patient can move safely and wear comfortable clothing. If relevant, have discharge advice or medical reports available to discuss at the visit; they need not be sent in the initial message.

4. Assessment and plan

Eissya discusses goals and assesses relevant movement and function. Treatment and home exercise guidance depend on the findings. If medical review or another service is needed, she can explain the next step.

Questions families ask

Can a caregiver join the session?

Yes, with the patient's agreement. Caregiver input can help connect the plan to everyday routines.

Do you visit outside Ipoh?

No. Home visits are limited to Ipoh, Perak.
