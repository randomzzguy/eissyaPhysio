## Search and content observations

- Search results for Ipoh home visits include both clinics and referral platforms. Dedicated stroke and elderly pages appear in competitors' navigation or articles, supporting distinct search intent for those pages.
- Prime Motion offers more service depth and clinic-based resources than a solo provider. Eissya's defensible value is continuity with one person and practical care in the patient's home.
- Several reviewed pages ask users to enquire for price; Eissya can show RM140, RM400 and RM1,000 totals up front, with the variable transport fee explained.
- We did not verify review counts, ratings, complaints, backlink profiles, indexed page totals or Core Web Vitals for these businesses. Do not infer poor service or customer dissatisfaction from that absence. A Google Maps and review audit should be completed manually with dated screenshots before making reputation comparisons.

## Questions worth answering better

How much is the full visit after transport? Can an adult child book for a parent? What happens in the first session? How does a caregiver participate? Which parts of stroke care are physiotherapy versus other disciplines? What happens if the patient cannot walk? The new pages answer these without outcome promises.
