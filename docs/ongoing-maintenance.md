# Ongoing SEO and content maintenance

## Monthly checks

1. In Search Console, record clicks, impressions, average position, top queries, indexed canonical URLs, sitemap status and Core Web Vitals. Record the reporting period and distinguish measured numbers from estimates.
2. Track WhatsApp CTA clicks only after an approved analytics and consent setup. Compare enquiries and confirmed bookings using a private, minimal-data log; clicks are not bookings.
3. Check the nine public URLs, canonical links, sitemap, robots file and WhatsApp number. Review mobile layout and third-party widget behaviour after site or browser changes.
4. Review package price and transport wording against the owner's current offer; update homepage, pricing, FAQ, `llms.txt` and schema together. The owner has instructed that the Trial Pack stays **RM400**.
5. Review Business Profile hours, service area, genuine photos, services and new reviews. Respond without revealing patient information.

## Quarterly checks

Revisit medical references and clinical content with Eissya, update review dates where a real review occurs, archive obsolete information, and decide whether an additional condition page answers a distinct search intent. Audit competitors using dated evidence; do not claim their reviews or pricing changed without checking. Re-run mobile Lighthouse and compare field data where available.

## Owner-controlled accounts

Google Business Profile, Search Console, analytics and any citation accounts should be owned by Eissya, with role-based access if collaborators help. Keep patient records and health details out of analytics, public reviews and ordinary SEO documents.
