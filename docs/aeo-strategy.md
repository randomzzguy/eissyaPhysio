# Answer-engine visibility strategy

## What is implemented

The public pages answer common questions in plain HTML, identify Nur Siti Eissya and the Ipoh-only service, state package totals and transport terms, and link to one another. `robots.txt` allows `OAI-SearchBot` and does not block Googlebot or Bingbot. [OpenAI's crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) distinguishes search crawling from other bots. The file makes no special GPTBot restriction; the owner can decide a separate training-crawler preference. `llms.txt` is an optional directory, not a ranking signal or substitute for normal indexing.

## Entity and evidence rules

The site consistently describes one independent physiotherapist, her provided degree, prior employment dates, service area and real contact number. No physical clinic, professional registration, specialist designation, review score or recovery outcome is asserted. Service schema links to the same visible facts. The stroke and older-adult pages cite [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/stroke), [stroke clinical guidance](https://www.strokeguideline.org/chapter/organisation-of-stroke-services/) and [WHO falls guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/falls) where relevant. Clinical wording needs Eissya's review before production release.
