# Ninety-day content calendar

No medical article is published by this implementation. Eissya must clinically review, date and approve each draft before it goes live. Every article should show author, reviewer, publication/review dates, relevant primary medical references, a suitable service link and an optional WhatsApp CTA. No generic exercise prescription without assessment.
