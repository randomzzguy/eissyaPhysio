## Cannibalisation rules

The homepage targets the broad brand plus "home physiotherapy in Ipoh" proposition. The home service page answers the detailed booking intent. Stroke and elderly pages each address distinct populations, with links back to the home service page. Pricing owns package queries. Do not create near-identical pages for "mobile", "at home" and "home visit" variants. Do not make location pages outside Ipoh.
