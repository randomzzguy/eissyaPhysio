# Local SEO and Google Business Profile guide — 30 October 2026

## Service-area profile

[Google's current guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) say a business that travels to customers can be eligible for a Business Profile. Eissya should confirm the business meets all applicable requirements, then create **one** owner-controlled profile. Use the real-world name **Eissya Physio**, choose the most accurate available physiotherapy category, set **Ipoh** as the service area, add `https://eissya.site/` and verify `+60 10-389 0319` before entry. Do not add surrounding towns. If the business operates from a residence without customer visits, hide its address. Add only real operating hours, genuine photos with consent, and supported services. The owner must control and complete verification; no profile was created here.

Suggested description for owner review: “Eissya Physio provides one-to-one home physiotherapy within Ipoh, Perak. Nur Siti Eissya works with adults and families on movement and mobility goals, including ongoing rehabilitation after stroke, older-adult mobility and musculoskeletal concerns. Visits include an individual assessment and a plan suited to the patient's needs. Communication is available in English and Bahasa Malaysia. Contact Eissya directly to discuss suitability, availability and the transport fee for your Ipoh location.”

Do not add prices, links or promotional offers to the Business Profile description; Google's [description rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) restrict those. Verify the selected category in the live interface rather than inventing an exact category label.

## Review workflow

Ask **all** eligible patients for an honest review after care, regardless of whether feedback was positive or negative. Ask privately, without pressure or incentives, and only after confirming the patient is comfortable posting publicly. Do not disclose a patient's condition when responding.

**Request:** “Thank you for allowing me to support your physiotherapy. If you are comfortable, would you share an honest Google review of your experience? Your feedback helps other Ipoh families understand the service. Please share only what you wish to make public. [Owner's verified review link]”

**One follow-up:** “Just a gentle reminder about the optional review link I sent. There is no obligation. Thank you again.”

**Response to positive review:** “Thank you for taking the time to share your experience. I appreciate your feedback.”

**Response to concern:** “Thank you for the feedback. I take concerns seriously and would welcome a private conversation at our published contact number so I can understand and address this.”

Never confirm publicly whether a reviewer was a patient or discuss their health information. Record request date, link sent, and opt-out status in a private, access-controlled workflow; do not copy medical details into a review tracking sheet.
