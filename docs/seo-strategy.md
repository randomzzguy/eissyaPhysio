# SEO strategy

## Position and architecture

Eissya Physio is a named, independent provider of one-to-one home physiotherapy within Ipoh. The homepage is the broad entry and conversion page. `/home-physiotherapy-ipoh/` handles detailed service intent; stroke and older-adult pages address the two most important patient groups; `/pricing/`, `/how-home-physiotherapy-works/`, `/about/`, `/faq/` and `/contact/` resolve booking questions and trust needs.

The current site does not need one page per keyword or each neighbourhood. Secondary condition pages and articles remain a clinical review backlog. Each new page needs a distinct question to answer, enough accurate detail, a visible author/reviewer where medical advice is involved, and a link to the relevant service page.

## Conversion path

Search result → clear page answer → named provider and realistic scope → visible package total and transport policy → WhatsApp enquiry. The first message asks for an Ipoh location and broad concern, not sensitive records. No claim that a response time, clinical outcome or booking slot is guaranteed.

## Trust and local signals

Use consistent name, phone and Ipoh-only service area on site and legitimate listings. Share degree and dates accurately; do not assert MAHPC registration, APC, specialist status, reviews or awards without evidence. A service-area Google Business Profile is the highest priority off-site task if the owner confirms eligibility and completes verification.

## Measurement

After approved deployment, establish Search Console and compare organic clicks, impressions, queries, indexed pages and mobile performance monthly. Track WhatsApp link clicks only after analytics and privacy decisions are made. Separate clicks from actual booked patients. Review page-level engagement and enquiry quality before adding further pages.
