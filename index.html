Hi, I'm Nur Siti Eissya — a physiotherapist with approximately 1.5 years of clinical experience with musculoskeletal, geriatric, neurological and pain-related concerns in Ipoh.

I believe recovery shouldn't be held back by logistics. That's why I bring the clinic to you — designing progressive, personalised rehabilitation programs in the environment where you'll actually do your exercises and live your daily life.

Whether you are seeking help with mobility after a stroke, moving with more confidence as you age, or managing pain, we can discuss goals that suit your circumstances. Read more about Eissya.
